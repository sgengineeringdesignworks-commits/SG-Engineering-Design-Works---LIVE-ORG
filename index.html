ABOUT US

SG Engineering & Design Works is a professional engineering design and drafting company providing Mechanical & Electrical Design Solutions for industrial, manufacturing, fabrication and infrastructure projects.

Our services include AutoCAD Drafting, 2D CAD Drafting, 3D Modeling, Mechanical Design, Electrical Design, Structural Steel Detailing, MEP Electrical Drafting, Industrial Plant Layouts, PLC & SCADA Drawings and Engineering Documentation.

We also provide Fabrication Drawings, Shop Drawings, GA Drawings, Fixture Design, Trolley Design, Reverse Engineering, Layout Planning, BOQ Preparation and As-Built Documentation.

Our focus is on delivering accurate, practical and production-ready engineering drawings that help clients improve project efficiency, reduce development time and successfully execute their projects.
